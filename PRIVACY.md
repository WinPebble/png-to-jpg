# WinPebble PNG to JPG Privacy Policy

Draft for version 2 — October 16, 2026.

WinPebble PNG to JPG converts PNG images to JPG locally on your Windows computer.

## Files you select

The app reads PNG files you select in File Explorer and creates JPG files beside
them. Images and filenames may contain personal information. The app does not
upload or transmit selected files, image contents or filenames to WinPebble or
another service.

Original PNG files are preserved. Existing JPG files are not replaced; a numbered
filename is used instead. Temporary output files are used during conversion and
cleanup is attempted when processing finishes or fails. Your images remain under
your control. Uninstalling the app does not delete the images you created.

## Collection and sharing

The app does not collect telemetry or usage analytics, show advertisements, use
tracking technologies, require a WinPebble account or run a background service.
Image conversion does not require an internet connection.

Microsoft Store and Windows may process information under Microsoft's privacy
policies when you acquire, install or update software or use Windows features.

## Support

For questions about this policy or the app, use the
[WinPebble PNG to JPG support tracker](https://github.com/WinPebble/png-to-jpg/issues).
GitHub issues are public. Do not include private images or personal information
in an issue. GitHub handles information you provide there under its own privacy
policy.
